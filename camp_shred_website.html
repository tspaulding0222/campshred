About The Event

This isn't some corporate event — it's 100% BY the racers, FOR the racers.

This is it — Camp Shred is back for Year 3 and it's going bigger, faster, and rowdier than ever!

Real riders. Real racing. Real community.

Camp Shred is all about pushing limits, building stoke, and riding with the best crew out there. Bring your board. Bring your A-game. Bring your party pants.

Location

Horns Hill Park

1250 Horns Hill Road Northeast
Newark, OH 43055

Nestled in the rolling hills of Ohio, Horns Hill Park offers the perfect terrain for riding and camping. The park features challenging trails, beautiful scenery, and plenty of space to set up camp.

Facilities include restrooms, water stations, and picnic areas.
